# The MatterCAD Menu on the Website

While you edit a design on the website, the toolbar starts with a **MatterCAD** menu (☰), before **Add to Bed**. On a phone the button shows only the ☰, at the start of the toolbar.

It holds five items: **Add System File to Bed**, **New Design**, **Interface Tour**, **Help** and **Settings**.

<!-- IMAGE_NEEDED: The website's editor with the MatterCAD menu open under its ☰ MatterCAD button at the start of the toolbar, showing Add System File to Bed, New Design, Interface Tour, Help and Settings -->

## Add System File to Bed

**Add System File to Bed** brings in a file from your computer or phone. Your browser asks you to pick the file, and its parts are added to the bed. See [Adding Parts on the Web](adding-parts.md#files-from-your-computer).

## New Design

**New Design** starts a new design on an empty bed, on the same page, and you keep editing.

If the design you have open has changes that are not saved, MatterCAD asks first, in a box titled **Save Changes?**: "Save your changes before you start a new design?"

- **Save Changes** - Saves the design, then starts the new one once the save has finished.
- **Discard Changes** - Starts the new design without saving your changes.
- **Cancel** - Keeps the design you have open, with its changes.

The new design is called "New Design" and the page's title changes to match. The title bar's **Download** button is hidden until you save it, because nothing is added to your library until then. The first **Save** opens **Save As** in the folder of the design you had open (or **My Designs** when that design wasn't in your own library), where you choose the folder and the name ([Saving on the Web](saving.md#save-as)).

To go back to the design you had open before, use your browser's Back button. It opens that design as it was last saved.

You can also start a new design from the website's **Library** page: open a folder and click **New Design**. MatterCAD opens on an empty bed, ready to edit, and the first **Save** opens **Save As** in that folder. If you press **Done** before you ever save it, you go back to the folder.

## Interface Tour

**Interface Tour** opens the **Welcome to MatterCAD** box. Click **Next** to start a tour that points out the parts of the editor one at a time, or **Done** to close the box. On the website the tour visits only the parts the website's editor has.

## Help

**Help** opens MatterCAD's help pages in a window over the editor. Your design stays as it is underneath.

The first time you open it, the articles take a moment to arrive: the window shows "Loading help…" until they do. If they can't be loaded, it says "Help couldn't be loaded. Check your internet connection, then open Help again." Opening **Help** again tries once more. The **Mouse** and **Keys** pages, which list every mouse control and shortcut, are there either way, even without a connection.

- On a wider screen the window opens at the right. You can move it, and close it with the close button in its title bar.
- On a phone, or in a narrow window, it covers the whole screen. Close it with the close button in its title bar.

## Settings

**Settings** opens a window with the settings that matter while you edit in a browser:

- **Number format** (under **General**) - How numbers show and are typed: **System** (your computer's or phone's own way), **1234.5** (a point before the decimals) or **1234,5** (a comma before the decimals). The editor redraws every number in the new format at once.
- **Depth Peeling Layers** (under **Options**) - How many layers of see-through parts the 3D view draws: **Off**, **4**, **6** (the starting setting) or **8**. Fewer layers draw faster, which can help on a slower phone.

The other settings MatterCAD has on your computer aren't in this window. The colors follow the light or dark theme you chose on the website, and the camera, snapping and units are set with the camera controls in the 3D view ([Viewport Navigation](../getting-started/viewport-navigation.md)).
