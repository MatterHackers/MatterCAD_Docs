# The MatterCAD Menu on the Website

While you edit a design on the website, the title bar above the design shows a **☰ MatterCAD** button, right after **View** and before the design's name. Click it to open the MatterCAD menu over the editor. Click it again, press Esc or click away to close the menu. The button is hidden while you only view the design.

It holds four items: **New Design**, **Interface Tour**, **Help** and **Settings**.

To add a file from your computer or phone, use **Add to Bed** and open **System File** ([Adding Parts on the Web](adding-parts.md#files-from-your-computer)). To send feedback, use the **Feedback** button in the website's header ([Getting More Help](../get-support.md#feedback-on-the-website)).

## New Design

**New Design** starts a new design on an empty bed, on the same page, and you keep editing.

If the design you have open has changes that are not saved, MatterCAD asks first, in a box titled **Save Changes?**: "Save your changes before you start a new design?"

- **Save Changes** - Saves the design, then starts the new one once the save has finished.
- **Discard Changes** - Starts the new design without saving your changes.
- **Cancel** - Keeps the design you have open, with its changes.

The new design is called "New Design" and the page's title changes to match. The title bar's **Download** button is hidden until you save it, because nothing is added to your library until then. The first **Save** opens **Save As** in the folder of the design you had open (or **My Designs** when that design wasn't in your own library), where you choose the folder and the name ([Saving on the Web](saving.md#save-as)).

To go back to the design you had open before, use your browser's Back button. It opens that design as it was last saved.

You can also start a new design from the website's **Library** page: open a folder and click **New Design**. MatterCAD opens on an empty bed, ready to edit, and the first **Save** opens **Save As** in that folder. If you press **View** before you ever save it, you go back to the folder.

## Interface Tour

**Interface Tour** opens the **Welcome to MatterCAD** box. Click **Next** to start a tour that points out the parts of the editor one at a time, or **Done** to close the box. On the website the tour visits only the parts the website's editor has.

## Help

**Help** opens MatterCAD's help pages on the website in a new browser tab, starting at the help's first page. The editor stays open in its own tab with your design as it is: switch back to that tab to keep working.

## Settings

**Settings** opens a window with the settings that matter while you edit in a browser:

- **Number format** (under **General**) - How numbers show and are typed: **System** (your computer's or phone's own way), **1234.5** (a point before the decimals) or **1234,5** (a comma before the decimals). The editor redraws every number in the new format at once.
- **Anti-aliasing** (under **3D View**) - Smooths jagged edges in the 3D view: **Auto** (the starting setting), **Off**, **2x**, **3x** or **4x**. **Auto** shows what it uses now, for example "Auto (2x)", and lowers it while you move the view if drawing gets slow. Higher settings use more graphics memory; a choice that doesn't fit the window is greyed out.
- **Transparency Layers** (under **3D View**) - How many overlapping see-through surfaces the 3D view draws: **Auto** (the starting setting), **Off**, **4**, **6** or **8**. Fewer is faster, which can help on a slower phone. **Auto** shows what it uses now, for example "Auto (6)", and lowers it while you move the view if drawing gets slow.

The other settings MatterCAD has on your computer aren't in this window. The colors follow the theme you chose on the website, in the menu under your name in the website's header (or the theme button beside **Sign in** when you aren't signed in), and the camera, snapping and units are set with the camera controls in the 3D view ([Viewport Navigation](../getting-started/viewport-navigation.md)).
