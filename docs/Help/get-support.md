# Issue Reporting & Support

To tell us about a problem, ask for a feature or ask a question, send feedback right from MatterCAD or from the MatterCAD website. For anything else, contact our support team at [MatterHackers Support](https://www.matterhackers.com/support).

## Submit Feedback in MatterCAD

Open the **Submit Feedback** window in either of these ways:

- Click **Submit Feedback** in the status bar at the bottom of the window, left of the theme button. In a narrow window the button shows only its icon.
- Choose **Submit Feedback...** in the **☰ MatterCAD** menu ([Menus and Toolbar](workspace/menus-and-toolbar.md)). On a Mac it is also in the menu bar's **Help** menu.

Feedback is sent with your MatterHackers account. If you aren't signed in, the window says "Sign in to send feedback" and offers **Sign In** first.

The window asks for:

- **The type** - **Bug**, **Feature request** or **Question**.
- **Title** - A short summary.
- **Description** - What happened, and what you expected. Near the 4,000-character limit the window shows how many characters are left.
- **Include with your report:** - Tick what goes with it: a **Screenshot of the 3D view**, **Your design** (the whole design, so we can reproduce the problem) and **Diagnostics** (MatterCAD's version, your system and graphics, your recent steps and the end of MatterCAD's log, with your computer's user name and your sign-in removed). Click **Show** to see the diagnostics before you send them.
- **OK to contact me about this (optional, we usually don't reply)** - Tick it if you don't mind us writing to you about your feedback. It starts unticked.

Click **Send**. When it arrives, the window says "Thank you. Your feedback was sent." with a reference you can copy with **Copy Reference**. If it couldn't be sent, the window says so and offers **Retry**.

## Feedback on the Website

The MatterCAD website has a **Feedback** button in its header, at the top right. What it opens depends on where you are:

- **On a design's page**, once MatterCAD has started, it opens MatterCAD's **Submit Feedback** window over the design, whether you are viewing the design or editing it. It is the same window described above, so your feedback can include a screenshot of the 3D view, your design and diagnostics.
- **Anywhere else** (the Library, Pricing or Help pages), it opens a short form titled **Send feedback about the website**: the kind of feedback (**Bug**, **Feature request** or **Question**), **Title**, **Description** and **OK to contact me**. Click **Send** to send it.

If you aren't signed in, the website asks you to sign in first, so MatterHackers can follow up with you.
