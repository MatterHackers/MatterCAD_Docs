# Subtract and Replace

Subtract and Replace is now one setting of the [Boolean](boolean.md) operation rather than an operation of its own. The toolbar button still says Subtract & Replace -- it creates a Boolean with that operation already chosen.

See **[Boolean](boolean.md)** for how to use it, including the **Part(s) to Subtract** setting and why the operation is not offered for 2D paths.

This page also covers the older Subtract and Replace objects still found in designs saved before the operations were merged. They keep working exactly as they did; new designs use [Boolean](boolean.md).
