# Mechanical Parts

The Library's **Included > Mechanical** folder holds parametric mechanical parts: gears, threads and screws.

## Tools

|Involute Gear|Threads|
| :--- | :--- |
|<!-- AUTO_IMAGE: type=from_thumbnail item=gear file=mechanical_gears -->![mechanical_gears](https://matterhackers.github.io/MatterCAD_Docs/assets/mechanical_gears.png)|<!-- AUTO_IMAGE: type=from_thumbnail item=threads file=mechanical_threads -->![mechanical_threads](https://matterhackers.github.io/MatterCAD_Docs/assets/mechanical_threads.png)|

- [Involute Gear](gears.md) - Spur gears, racks and internal gears with configurable teeth, circular pitch and pressure angle, as a solid or a 2D outline
- [Threads](threads.md) - A threaded rod, or the threaded hole it fits
- [Screw](screw.md) - A complete fastener from a standard metric or inch size, or the hole for one
- [Cycloidal Gear](cycloidal-gear.md) - Generate the disc and pin ring of a cycloidal speed reducer
