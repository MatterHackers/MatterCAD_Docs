# Managing Your AI Plan

You manage your MatterHackers AI plan on your **Plan page** on mattercad.com: [mattercad.com/account/plan](https://mattercad.com/account/plan). There you can change your plan, change the card it renews with, cancel, resume, and see your past orders.

To open it:

- In MatterCAD, click the meter at the bottom of the **AI** tab, then **Manage plan**. It opens the Plan page in your web browser.
- On the pricing page, while you pay for a plan, click **Change plan** on any other plan.
- Or go to [mattercad.com/account/plan](https://mattercad.com/account/plan) and sign in with your MatterHackers account.

To see what each plan includes, see [AI Plans](ai-plans.md).

## What the Plan Page Shows

The Plan page shows your plan's name, whether you pay **Monthly** or **Yearly** and its price, and one line saying where your plan stands:

- **Renews** and a date, with the price - Your plan renews on that day.
- **Changes to** another plan **on** a date - A change you chose starts that day (see [Move to a Smaller Plan](#move-to-a-smaller-plan-or-switch-monthly-and-yearly)).
- **Ends** and a date, "It won't renew." - You cancelled. Your plan works until that day.
- "We couldn't charge your card." - A renewal payment didn't go through (see [When a Payment Fails](#when-a-payment-fails)).
- **Ended** and a date - The plan is over.

Under it, **Renews with** names the card your plan renews with, for example "Visa ending 4242, expires 04/29". The buttons under that are the changes you can make now.

If you don't have a plan, the page says "You're on Free." with a **See plans** button. If MatterHackers gave you a plan, the page says so, for example "MatterHackers gave you Plus until Nov 29, 2026."

Prices on the Plan page are in US dollars, plus sales tax.

Every change asks first. The page shows what will happen and what you will be charged, and for a change to what you pay, the new terms beside a box to tick. Nothing changes until you click the button that makes the change. To go back without changing anything, click **Keep my plan** (or **Not now**).

## Move to a Bigger Plan

A bigger plan starts right away, so its bigger weekly allowance is yours at once. Moving from Plus to Max, or from Max 5× to Max 10×, is a bigger plan. So is moving from Plus paid yearly to Max.

1. Click **Change plan**. Under **Choose a plan**, each plan you can move to is listed with its price; yours is marked **Your plan**.
2. Click **Choose** beside the plan you want.
3. The page says the new plan starts now, what you are charged today with its sales tax, the refund for the unused part of your current plan, and when the new plan renews after that.
4. Tick the box to agree to the new terms, and click **Change my plan**.

The page then says, for example, "You're on Max 5× now.", what was charged, and the refund on its way to your card.

## Move to a Smaller Plan, or Switch Monthly and Yearly

A smaller plan, or a switch between paying monthly and yearly, starts when your plan next renews. You keep your current plan, and its allowance, until then, and nothing is charged today.

1. Click **Change plan**, then **Choose** beside the plan you want.
2. The page says, for example, "Changes to Plus monthly on Nov 29, 2026, when your plan renews." and "Nothing is charged today.", then the new terms.
3. Tick the box and click **Change my plan**.

Your Plan page now says **Changes to** that plan **on** that day.

If you change your mind before then, click **Keep** and your plan's name, for example **Keep Max 10× monthly**. The page asks first ("You'll keep Max 10× monthly, and won't change to Plus monthly."); tick the box and click the button to keep your plan as it is.

## Change Your Card

1. Click **Change card**. Under **Renew with**, the cards you have saved are listed, with **Your card now** beside the one your plan uses.
2. Either click **Use this card** beside another saved card, or, under **A new card**, enter the card and your billing address and click **Save card**.

The page says "Your new card is saved. Your plan renews with it." Click **Keep my card** to go back without changing it.

## Cancel Your Plan

1. Click **Cancel**.
2. The page says your plan stays until the end of the period you paid for and won't renew. Click **Cancel my plan**, or **Keep my plan** to keep it.

You keep your plan, and its allowance, until that day. Your card isn't charged again. Your Plan page now says **Ends** and the day. After that day you are back on Free; everything else in MatterCAD keeps working, and so do your designs.

## Resume Your Plan

Changed your mind after cancelling? Before your plan ends, you can have it renew again:

1. Click **Resume**.
2. The page says "Your plan will renew again." and the terms it renews under. Tick the box and click **Resume my plan**.

The page then says when your plan will renew. **Resume** needs a card to renew with; it is offered while your plan still has one.

Once your plan has ended, it can't be resumed. Click **See plans** and buy a plan again (see [Buying an AI Plan](buying-an-ai-plan.md)).

## When a Payment Fails

If your card can't be charged when your plan renews, the Plan page says "We couldn't charge your card." with the day we'll try again, and asks you to update your card to keep your plan. Until then you can only change your card or cancel; changing your plan waits until the payment goes through.

To keep your plan, click **Change card** and choose or enter a card that works. The new card is charged right away, and the page says what was charged, sales tax included.

If you would rather stop, click **Cancel**. Because the last payment didn't go through, your plan ends right away and your card won't be charged again.

## Receipts and Past Orders

Under **Past orders**, the Plan page lists each order for your plan with its date, total and order number, and whether it was paid or refunded. Click **Receipt** to see the receipt on matterhackers.com.

## If a Change Doesn't Go Through

While a change goes through, the page says "We're confirming your change…". Wait for it. If it can't confirm the change, it asks you to reload the page in a few minutes to see whether it went through. Other problems say what to do, for example "Your card was declined, so your plan hasn't changed. Try another card."
