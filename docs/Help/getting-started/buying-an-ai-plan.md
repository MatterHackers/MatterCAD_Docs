# Buying an AI Plan

You buy a MatterHackers AI plan with your MatterHackers account. Start from the **AI** tab in MatterCAD, from the pricing page at [mattercad.com/pricing](https://mattercad.com/pricing), or from the MatterHackers store on matterhackers.com. Whichever you use, the plan goes to your account, so MatterCAD finds it once you are signed in with the same account.

To see what each plan includes first, see [AI Plans](ai-plans.md). Prices are on the pricing page, in US dollars, plus sales tax where it applies. Plans are available to customers in the US for now.

## From the AI Tab

When you don't have a plan yet, the **AI** tab shows the plans under the heading "Design with MatterHackers AI". Each card shows the plan's name, its price (and, for a plan you can pay yearly, its yearly price), what it includes, and a **Get** button. The plan MatterHackers recommends is marked **Recommended**. On the Max card, click **5×** or **10×** to choose its size.

1. Click **Get Plus**, or **Get Max**. The pricing page opens in your web browser, at that plan's card.
2. Buy the plan there: see [Buy on the Pricing Page](#buy-on-the-pricing-page).
3. Come back to MatterCAD. As the line under the cards says, "You'll finish on mattercad.com. Once you subscribe, the chat opens here by itself." Shortly after, the plans go away and the chat says, for example, "You're on Plus."

If you are signed out on your computer, the AI tab asks you to sign in and shows the plans under that. Click **Sign In** and sign in with the MatterHackers account you buy the plan with (see [Signing In](../workspace/account.md)).

## Buy on the Pricing Page

1. Open [mattercad.com/pricing](https://mattercad.com/pricing). At the top, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**. Yearly changes the price only for a plan you can pay yearly; Max is monthly only.
2. Click **Get** on the plan you want. Sign in to your MatterHackers account if the page asks you to.
3. The checkout page shows the plan, its price, how you are billed ("Billed today, then every month until you cancel.", or every year), what is included, and who is signed in. If that isn't you, click **Sign in with another account**.
4. Enter your card and your US billing address: name, street, city, state and ZIP code. Plans are paid by card.
5. Click **Continue**. The page shows the plan's price, the sales tax, the **Total today**, and when the plan renews.
6. Read the terms beside the box, tick it, and click **Subscribe**.

While the purchase goes through, the page says "We're confirming your purchase…". Wait for it; you won't be charged twice.

If something stops the purchase, the page says what to do. For example:

- "Your card was declined. Try another card or contact your bank."
- "Plans are available to US customers for now."
- "You already have Plus. You can change it on your Plan page." - To move to another plan, see [Managing Your AI Plan](managing-your-ai-plan.md).

If you already have a plan, the pricing page marks it **Your plan** and the other plans have a **Change plan** button, which opens your Plan page.

If clicking **Get** says "Plans open soon", plans aren't on sale yet. Leave your email address and click **Notify me**, and you'll get an email when that plan is available.

## Buy on matterhackers.com

The plans are also sold in the MatterHackers store on matterhackers.com. Sign in to the store with the same MatterHackers account you use in MatterCAD, so the plan reaches your account. Once you have bought it, it shows on your Plan page on mattercad.com and in MatterCAD's **AI** tab.

## After You Buy

The page after the checkout says, for example, "You're on Plus. MatterHackers AI is ready in MatterCAD." Then:

1. Open MatterCAD, signed in with the same MatterHackers account.
2. Open the **AI** tab and start designing. MatterCAD picks up your plan the next time it checks. If it still shows your old plan, wait a minute and look again.

Your plan works the same in MatterCAD on your computer and on mattercad.com. The meter at the bottom of the **AI** tab now shows your plan's weekly allowance (see [The Weekly AI Allowance](ai-plans.md#the-weekly-ai-allowance)).

The MatterHackers store handles the payment and sends your plan's emails. Each order is listed under **Past orders** on your Plan page, with a **Receipt** link that opens it on matterhackers.com. To change your plan, change your card or cancel, see [Managing Your AI Plan](managing-your-ai-plan.md).

## Have a Code?

If you have a MatterHackers AI code, use it on your Plan page at [mattercad.com/account/plan](https://mattercad.com/account/plan):

- A code on a MatterHackers store receipt adds its time to your plan.
- A gift code from MatterHackers gives you its plan.

1. Under **Have a code?**, type the code into **Code**.
2. Click **Use code**.

The page says what the code gave you, for example "Your code added 1 month of Plus." or "Your code gives you Plus until Nov 29, 2026." A plan from a store code shows "Prepaid until" and its last day, and doesn't renew by itself.

If the code can't be used, the page says why, for example "That code has already been used." or "We don't know that code. Check it, then try again."
