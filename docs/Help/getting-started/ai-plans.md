# AI Plans

MatterCAD is free. A plan for MatterHackers AI adds an AI that designs with you in the [AI tab](ai-tab.md), and more room in your [Cloud Library](../library/cloud-library.md).

You need a plan only for MatterHackers AI. Everything else in MatterCAD works without one. If you use your own Anthropic or OpenAI API keys instead (see [Settings](../workspace/settings.md#ai-providers)), you don't need a plan, and the allowance on this page doesn't apply.

See the prices on the pricing page, [mattercad.com/pricing](https://mattercad.com/pricing). Prices are in US dollars, plus sales tax where it applies. Plans are available to customers in the US for now.

To get a plan, see [Buying an AI Plan](buying-an-ai-plan.md). To change or cancel one, see [Managing Your AI Plan](managing-your-ai-plan.md).

## The Plans

- **Free** - All of MatterCAD, on your computer and on the website, and some cloud storage. While MatterHackers offers it, Free also comes with a one-time starter AI allowance (see [The Free Starter](#the-free-starter)); the Free card on the pricing page says so when it does.
- **Plus** - For everyday designing. AI chat, pictures and 3D models with a weekly AI allowance, more cloud storage, in MatterCAD and on mattercad.com. You can pay for Plus monthly or yearly.
- **Max 5×** - Everything in Plus, with 5 times the AI use of Plus each week, and more cloud storage again.
- **Max 10×** - Everything in Plus, with 10 times the AI use of Plus each week, and the most cloud storage.

On the pricing page, Max is one card: choose its size, **5×** or **10×**. Max is paid monthly. The **Compare plans** table under the cards lists each plan's price, its AI use compared with Plus, and its cloud storage.

Your plan belongs to your MatterHackers account. Sign in with the same account in MatterCAD on Windows, macOS or Linux, and on mattercad.com, and your plan comes with you.

## The Weekly AI Allowance

A paid plan gives you one AI allowance for the week. Everything MatterHackers AI does for you uses it: chatting in the **AI** tab, making pictures and making 3D models all come out of the same allowance. There is no separate daily limit.

The allowance counts what you used over the last 7 days. It doesn't reset at midnight or on a set day of the week: as your older use passes 7 days, it stops counting, so room comes back a little at a time.

You see how much you have used at the bottom of the **AI** tab. The meter shows your plan's name, a bar, and your use as a percentage of the week, for example "Plus" and "30% of this week". The bar is green, turns amber once you have used more than 75% and red past 90%.

Click the meter for details. The card that opens is titled with your plan, for example "Your Plus plan". It shows how much of the last 7 days you have used and, once you have used some, when more room comes back, for example "More room at 3:40 PM (in about 3 hours)". If you pay for your plan, the **Manage plan** link at the bottom of the card opens your Plan page on mattercad.com (see [Managing Your AI Plan](managing-your-ai-plan.md)).

## When You Reach 100%

When you have used the whole week's allowance, the AI tab says so and when more is available, for example "You've used this week's Plus allowance. More is available in about 2 days." The meter's bar is full. Under the message is a card for the next plan up, if there is one, and a **See all plans** link to the pricing page.

You can then:

- **Wait.** As older use passes 7 days, room comes back, and the AI tab works again. The plan card goes away on its own.
- **Move to a bigger plan.** A bigger plan starts right away, so you can keep going now. See [Move to a Bigger Plan](managing-your-ai-plan.md#move-to-a-bigger-plan).

Everything else in MatterCAD keeps working while you wait, and your designs and chats stay as they are.

## The Free Starter

While MatterHackers offers it, the Free plan comes with a one-time starter allowance, so you can try MatterHackers AI before you choose a plan. It is for a MatterHackers account whose email address is confirmed. Until yours is, the AI tab says "Confirm your email address to try MatterHackers AI free."

- The meter reads "Free starter", with a bar and how much you have used, for example "25% used". Click it for the card titled "Your free starter".
- The starter is used once. It doesn't refill each week the way a plan's allowance does.
- When it is used up, the AI tab says "Your free starter allowance is used up. Choose a plan to keep designing with MatterHackers AI." and shows the plans, so you can choose one.

## Without a Plan

When you are signed in without a plan, and the chat would use MatterHackers AI, the AI tab says "Choose a plan to design with MatterHackers AI." and shows a card for each plan, with a button to get it. If you are signed out on your computer, it asks you to sign in and shows the plans under that. See [Buying an AI Plan](buying-an-ai-plan.md#from-the-ai-tab).
